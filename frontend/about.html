About CareerGuide

Career Guidance and Recommendation System using RIASEC Assessment

What is CareerGuide?

CareerGuide is a career guidance and recommendation system designed to help students understand their career interests and explore suitable career options.

The system uses the RIASEC assessment to identify a user's strongest career-interest areas and provide personalized career and course recommendations.

Our goal is to help students make better career decisions based on their interests, strengths and preferences.

Our Goal

To guide students toward suitable career paths by understanding their interests through the RIASEC model.

What is RIASEC?

RIASEC is a career-interest model developed by psychologist John L. Holland.

R

Realistic

Interested in practical, hands-on activities, machines, tools and physical work.

I

Investigative

Interested in research, analysis, science and problem-solving.

A

Artistic

Interested in creativity, design, writing, art and self-expression.

S

Social

Interested in teaching, helping, counseling and working with people.

E

Enterprising

Interested in leadership, business, persuasion and decision-making.

C

Conventional

Interested in organized work, records, numbers and structured procedures.
